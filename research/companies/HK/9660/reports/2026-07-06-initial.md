# Company Research: Horizon Robotics (HK:9660)

Date: 2026-07-06
Research Type: initial
Analyst: Codex

## One-line Conclusion

Horizon Robotics is a credible high-growth intelligent driving platform company, but at the current post-IPO valuation the margin of safety is still thin; it is a watchlist name, not a full-position buy.

## Decision

Watch. A small starter position is only reasonable near the lower end of the buy zone, and the main action today is to track execution rather than chase the stock.

## Business Understanding

Horizon Robotics sells automotive computing solutions for assisted and automated driving. Its core business is a combination of automotive-grade chips, software toolchains, algorithms, and reference solutions used by OEMs and Tier-1 partners. The company is not merely a chip vendor; the more important question is whether it can become a recurring platform embedded across vehicle programs.

The attractive part of the model is that successful automotive design wins can produce multi-year shipment visibility. The weak part is that OEM bargaining power is high, automotive qualification cycles are slow, and platform economics remain unproven while the company is still loss-making.

## Industry and Competitive Context

The Chinese intelligent driving stack is moving from high-end optional features toward mass-market ADAS. This expands the addressable market, but also compresses pricing because OEMs want lower-cost, localized solutions. Horizon benefits from localization, software integration, and domestic OEM relationships. It competes with Nvidia, Qualcomm, Mobileye, Huawei, in-house OEM efforts, and other local chip and system suppliers.

The key industry variable is not only ADAS penetration. It is whether OEMs standardize on a smaller number of compute platforms, and whether Horizon can keep enough pricing power as intelligent driving becomes a default configuration rather than a premium differentiator.

## Company Quality

The quality signal is mixed but improving. The company has real customers, real production programs, and a recognizable position in China's smart-vehicle supply chain. The 2025 annual report shows revenue scale and gross margin moving in the right direction. That suggests product-market fit is no longer purely speculative.

The quality discount is also obvious: Horizon is still burning money, has heavy R&D needs, faces rapid technology cycles, and depends on automaker program wins. A strong gross margin does not yet prove durable operating leverage.

## Financial Quality

For 2025, Horizon reported revenue of about RMB 3.758 billion, up about 67.6% year over year. Gross profit was about RMB 2.425 billion, with gross margin around 64.5%. Vehicle solution revenue was the dominant segment at about RMB 3.579 billion. The same annual report still showed a large adjusted net loss, so this is a growth-stage scaling story rather than an earnings compounder.

The current quality of earnings is therefore low in the Graham/Munger sense but acceptable for a venture-style public equity bucket. The next proof point is whether revenue growth can remain above 40% while adjusted operating losses narrow materially.

## Valuation

Reference market price used for this report: about HKD 4.47 per share, with market capitalization around HKD 65.3 billion, based on public quote snapshots checked on 2026-07-06.

My fair value range is HKD 4.80-6.80 per share. The range assumes Horizon can compound revenue at high double digits in the near term, sustain gross margin above 55%, and move toward operating leverage over the next several years. I am not assigning much value to far-out robotaxi optionality because current public evidence is stronger for production ADAS than for fully autonomous monetization.

The stock becomes genuinely interesting below HKD 4.40, where the downside from execution disappointment is better compensated. Above HKD 8.50, the market is paying for several years of smooth execution in advance.

## Price and Position Plan

Current action: watch.

Buy zone: HKD 3.60-4.40.

Fair value range: HKD 4.80-6.80.

Reduce zone: HKD 8.50-11.00.

Position plan:
- Above HKD 4.40: no new position unless a new report proves faster operating leverage.
- HKD 4.00-4.40: allow a starter position up to 2% of portfolio.
- HKD 3.60-4.00: allow up to 4% if revenue growth and gross margin remain intact.
- Below HKD 3.60: allow up to 6% only after checking whether the decline is valuation-driven rather than thesis-driven.

## Key Assumptions

- ADAS penetration in China continues to rise and moves into mass-market vehicle models.
- Horizon keeps meaningful domestic OEM relationships and converts design wins into volume shipments.
- Gross margin remains above 55% as revenue scales.
- Adjusted operating losses narrow over the next 4-6 reporting periods.
- The company avoids a major technical reset caused by competitors' chips or OEM in-house platforms.

## Follow-up Triggers

- 2026-08-31: review interim results and check whether revenue growth remains above 40%.
- 2026-11-30: review any Q3 operating update, major OEM design wins, or margin commentary.
- Price below HKD 4.40: re-check whether the stock is entering the buy zone for valuation reasons.
- Price above HKD 8.50: re-check whether valuation has outrun confirmed operating leverage.

## Risks

- Automotive customers pressure pricing as intelligent driving becomes standardized.
- A major OEM shifts to in-house or competitor compute platforms.
- Gross margin falls as Horizon chases volume.
- R&D and sales spending keep losses elevated longer than expected.
- Public-market enthusiasm for AI hardware reverses and compresses revenue multiples.
